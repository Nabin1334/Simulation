Understanding Acceleration Physics

What is Acceleration?

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with time. In simple terms, it's how quickly something speeds up or slows down.

a = (v₂ - v₁) ÷ t

Where v₂=final speed, v₁=initial speed, t=time

Real Bus Physics

A typical school bus accelerates at 0.8-1.5 m/s². This simulation uses realistic physics:

  • Mass: 8000 kg (realistic bus weight)
  • Max acceleration: 2.0 m/s²
  • Max deceleration: 2.5 m/s²

Calculation Example

From 10 km/h to 30 km/h (2.8 to 8.3 m/s) in 5 seconds:
Acceleration = (8.3 - 2.8) ÷ 5
Acceleration = 1.1 m/s²

a = (8.3 - 2.8) ÷ 5 = 1.1 m/s²
