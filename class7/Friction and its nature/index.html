🔬 Physics Principles

1. Weight Force: Acts straight down

W = mg = 10 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 98 N

2. Normal Force: Perpendicular to surface

N = mg cos(θ)

3. Downhill Component: Along the slope

F_parallel = mg sin(θ)

4. Static Friction: Prevents motion

F_s ≤ μ_s × N

5. Kinetic Friction: Opposes motion

F_k = μ_k × N

🎯 Key Concepts

Critical Angle: The minimum angle where sliding begins

θ_critical = arctan(μ_s)

Static vs Kinetic Friction:

• Static friction (μ_s) is higher - harder to start moving

• Kinetic friction (μ_k) is lower - easier to keep moving

• This is why μ_k < μ_s always!

Net Force:

F_net = F_parallel - F_friction

Motion occurs when:

• F_parallel > F_s (at rest)

• Then acceleration a = F_net / m
