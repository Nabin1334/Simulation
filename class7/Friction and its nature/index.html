Physics Principles

Weight Force
W = m × g

The force due to gravity acting straight downward. In this simulation: m = 10 kg, g = 9.8 m/s²

Normal Force
N = m × g × cos(θ)

The force perpendicular to the surface. It decreases as the angle increases.

Downhill Force
F∥ = m × g × sin(θ)

The component of weight parallel to the surface. This force tries to pull the box downhill.

Friction Force
Ff = μ × N

The force opposing motion. Static friction (μs) prevents motion; kinetic friction (μk) opposes sliding.

Critical Angle
θcritical = arctan(μs)

The angle at which downhill force equals maximum static friction. Beyond this angle, the box will slide.

Net Force
Fnet = F∥ - Ff

The unbalanced force that causes acceleration. When net force > 0, the box accelerates downhill.
