How the Internet Works

The Internet is a global network connecting millions of computers worldwide! When you watch a video or visit a website, your request travels through this digital network to find what you need.

Think of it like sending a letter to a friend. You put it in an envelope (a data packet), and it goes through post offices (routers) until it reaches your friend's house (website server).

Fun Fact!

Did you know the internet was invented in the 1960s? It started as a way for scientists to share information between computers!

Super Fast!

Data on the internet can travel at nearly the speed of light! That's why you can watch videos from across the world instantly.

OSI Model - 7 Layers of Internet

7. Application - Where you use apps like browsers
6. Presentation - Data formatting and encryption
5. Session - Managing connections
4. Transport - Breaking data into packets
3. Network - Routing data between networks
2. Data Link - Local network communication
1. Physical - Actual hardware transmission
