Class 7 Internet Concepts

This simulation shows how data travels through the global internet infrastructure. Here are the key concepts:

1 Tier 1 Backbone (Red): Major data centers and undersea cables connecting continents
2 ISPs - Internet Service Providers (Green): Local providers that connect users to the backbone
3 Client Devices (Blue): Your computers, phones, and tablets that request and receive data
4 Data Packets: Small pieces of information that travel through the network (Yellow = Request, Cyan = Response)
5 Latency: The time it takes for data to travel - affected by distance and network congestion
