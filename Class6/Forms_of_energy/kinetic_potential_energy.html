What is Energy?

Energy is what makes things happen! It comes in different forms, but today we're learning about two special ones:

🔋
Potential Energy

Energy that is stored and waiting to be used!

⚡
Kinetic Energy

Energy of motion - when things are moving!

Roller Coaster

At the top: Lots of potential energy

Going down: Potential turns to kinetic!

Bouncing Ball

In your hand: Potential energy

Falling down: Kinetic energy!

Swing

At the highest point: Potential energy

Swinging down: Kinetic energy!
