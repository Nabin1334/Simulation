About Technology in Nepal

🏔️ Local Technology

Traditional tools and methods used for generations in Nepal. Made from natural materials like wood, stone, and clay.

  • Dhiki (rice beater)
  • Chulo (clay stove)
  • Kodalo (hoe)
  • Gagri (water pot)

🏙️ Modern Technology

New inventions that use electricity, machines, and computers to make life easier in Nepal.

  • Mobile phones
  • Solar panels
  • Tractors
  • Computers

🌟 Did You Know?

Many villages in Nepal now use both traditional and modern technologies together!

For example, people might cook on a gas stove but still use a dhiki for special occasions.
