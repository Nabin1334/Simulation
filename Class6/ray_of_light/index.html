About Volumetric Lighting

Volumetric light rays are visible due to light scattering off particles in the air. This effect is known as "crepuscular rays" or "god rays" in atmospheric optics.

The searchlight beam demonstrates how light spreads and becomes visible when interacting with atmospheric particles.
