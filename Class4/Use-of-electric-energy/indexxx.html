Electricity at Home

Our homes use electricity for lighting, cooking, cleaning, entertainment, and keeping food fresh. Without electricity, our homes would be very different!

Did You Know?

  • The average home has over 30 electrical devices
  • Refrigerators use the most electricity in most homes
  • LED lights use 75% less energy than old bulbs
  • Turning off devices when not in use saves energy
