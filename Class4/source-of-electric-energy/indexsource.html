Where Electricity Comes From

Electricity doesn't just appear in our outlets! It comes from many different sources that convert other forms of energy into electrical energy.

Solar Power

Solar panels capture energy from the sun and convert it into electricity. This is a clean, renewable energy source that doesn't create pollution!

Did You Know?

  • The sun provides enough energy in one hour to power the world for a year!
  • Wind power is one of the fastest-growing energy sources
  • Hydropower uses flowing water to spin turbines
  • Fossil fuels like coal and gas come from ancient plants and animals
