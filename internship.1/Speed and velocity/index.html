Speed and Velocity

Understanding the difference between scalar and vector quantities | Max Speed: 180 m/s

Understanding Concepts

What is Speed?

Speed tells us HOW FAST something is moving.

It's just a number that shows distance divided by time.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time
Example:

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours.
Speed = 120 km ÷ 2 h = 60 km/h

What is Velocity?

Velocity tells us HOW FAST and IN WHICH DIRECTION something is moving.

It's speed with direction added to it.

Velocity = Displacement ÷ Time
Example:

A plane flies 800 km North in 1 hour.
Velocity = 800 km North ÷ 1 h = 800 km/h North

Key Difference

Speed = Just a number (how fast)

Velocity = Number + Direction (how fast + which way)

Important:

If you run around a track and return to start:
• Your speed is not zero
• Your velocity is zero (no net direction)

Real World Examples

🚗

Car on Highway

Speed: 100 km/h

Velocity: 100 km/h East

Speedometer shows speed. Need compass for velocity.

🏃‍♀️

Runner on Track

Speed: 6 m/s (constant)

Velocity: Changes on curves!

Speed stays same, velocity changes with direction.

✈️

Airplane Flying

Speed: 800 km/h

Velocity: 800 km/h Northeast

Pilots need both speed and direction for navigation.

🚲

Bicycle Commute

Speed: 15 km/h

Velocity: 15 km/h towards school

Direction matters for getting to your destination.
