analog vs digital

🌊

analog

  • 📈 Continuous signal
  • 🎚️ Infinite possible values
  • 🌊 Smooth, curvy wave
  • 🔊 Human voice, old radio
  • 🌡️ Mercury thermometer
🎤 microphone 📻 AM/FM 🎚️ volume dial
⚡

digital

  • 📊 Discrete signal
  • 🎯 Only two values: 0 or 1
  • 🔲 Square wave (on/off)
  • 💻 Computers, smartphones
  • 💡 LED (on/off)
💽 USB drive ⌨️ keyboard 📀 CD/DVD
