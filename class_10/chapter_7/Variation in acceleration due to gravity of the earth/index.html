Why gravity varies:
• Earth's rotation creates centrifugal force (reduces g by ~0.34% at equator)
• Earth is oblate (flattened at poles, equatorial radius 21km larger)
• Altitude changes distance from center (g decreases by ~0.3%/km)
• Density variations in Earth's crust (geological structures)
