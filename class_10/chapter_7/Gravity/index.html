Gravity

This simulation demonstrates Gravitation:

F = G × (m₁ × m₂) / r²

Key Concepts:

• Acceleration: Gravity causes constant downward acceleration (9.8 m/s² on Earth)

• Velocity: Speed increases linearly with time: v = g × t

• Position: Distance fallen: d = ½ × g × t²

• Energy: Potential energy converts to kinetic energy

Simulation Physics:

• Gravity: -0.025 units/frame²

• Bounce restitution: 40% energy loss

• Rolling friction applied after impact
