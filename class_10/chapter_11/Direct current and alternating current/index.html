⚡ Direct Current (DC) Circuit with Physical Switch

In a DC circuit, current flows in one constant direction. The battery's positive terminal connects to the physical wall switch (shown in the middle of the circuit), then to the bulb's center contact through the hot wire (red). When the switch is OPEN, the circuit is broken and no current flows. When CLOSED, current passes through the switch contacts, through the tungsten filament, heating it to 2500°C to produce light. Click on the switch to toggle it ON/OFF.
