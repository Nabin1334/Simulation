Modes of Communication

Exchange of information shapes our world. Two fundamental modes: one‑way (broadcast) and two‑way (interactive).

Each fits different needs: mass media, private conversation, collaboration.

📺 one‑way

One‑way communication

Information flows from sender to receiver without immediate feedback.

  • TV / radio broadcasts
  • Newspapers, newsletters
  • Billboards, announcements
  • Passive consumption

efficient for large audiences, consistent message.

📱 two‑way

Two‑way communication

Interactive dialogue with continuous feedback.

  • Phone / video calls
  • Chat & messaging
  • Meetings & discussions
  • Social media interaction

builds understanding, immediate clarification.
