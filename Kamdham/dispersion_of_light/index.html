About This Simulation

This animation demonstrates the dispersion of white light into its constituent colors (the visible spectrum) when it passes through a glass prism.

How It Works:

White light enters the prism and slows down as it moves from air to glass (refraction). Different colors of light bend at slightly different angles because they travel at different speeds in glass. This separation of colors is called dispersion.

Physics Concepts:

  • Refraction: Bending of light when it passes from one medium to another
  • Dispersion: Separation of white light into its component colors
  • Wavelength: Different colors have different wavelengths (Red: 700nm, Violet: 400nm)
  • Speed Variation: Light travels at different speeds in different media
