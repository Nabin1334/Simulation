Physics Concepts

The displacement-time graph shows how far the bus has moved from its starting point over time.

Slope of the graph = Velocity

  • Positive slope: Bus moving forward (positive velocity)
  • Negative slope: Bus moving backward (negative velocity)
  • Zero slope: Bus is stationary (zero velocity)
  • Steeper slope: Higher speed
Displacement = Velocity × Time

Adjust the throttle to change velocity and observe how it affects the graph's shape.
