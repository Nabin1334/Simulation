📐 Equations of Linear Motion (SUVAT)
v = u + at
Final velocity = Initial velocity + (Acceleration × Time)
s = ut + ½at²
Displacement = (Initial velocity × Time) + (½ × Acceleration × Time²)
v² = u² + 2as
Velocity squared = Initial velocity² + (2 × Acceleration × Displacement)
