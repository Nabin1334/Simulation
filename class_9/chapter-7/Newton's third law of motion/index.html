How it works:

  1. When thrusters fire, fuel particles are expelled downward (action).
  2. The expelled fuel pushes the rocket upward with equal force (reaction).
  3. Greater thrust = more fuel particles = stronger reaction force.
  4. When fuel runs out, action stops and so does the reaction.
