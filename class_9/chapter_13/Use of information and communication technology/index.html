📺 Mass Media Information

What are Mass Media?

  • Communication channels reaching large audiences
  • Traditional forms: Print, Broadcast, Digital
  • Used for information, education, entertainment
  • Essential for public awareness and democracy

Types of Mass Media

  • Print Media: Newspapers, Magazines, Books
  • Broadcast Media: Radio, Television
  • Film & Cinema: Movies, Documentaries
  • Digital Media: Websites, Social Media

Nepali Context

  • Radio is vital in remote mountain areas
  • Educational TV programs for distance learning
  • Magazines in Nepali and English languages
  • Community radio for local information

Key Features

Wide Reach: Can inform millions simultaneously
Timely Information: News updates as events happen
Public Service: Emergency alerts, public announcements
Educational Role: Learning programs for students

📞 Communication Devices

How They Work

  • Voice Input: Sound converted to signals
  • Transmission: Through wires or wireless
  • Output: Signals converted back to sound
  • Network: Infrastructure connecting devices

Device Comparison

  • Telephone: Wired, reliable, emergency use
  • Fax: Document transmission, signatures
  • Mobile: Portable, multifunctional, internet
  • Satellite Phone: Works anywhere globally

Nepali Communication

  • Mobile network expansion to remote areas
  • Landlines still used in offices and businesses
  • SIM card registration system in place
  • Improving network coverage in mountains

Key Features

Two-way Communication: Interactive conversation between parties
Instant Connection: Real-time voice communication
Emergency Service: Critical for police, ambulance, fire
Business Essential: Professional communication tool

🌐 Digital Network

Internet Basics

  • Global network of interconnected computers
  • Uses TCP/IP protocols for communication
  • World Wide Web (WWW) is part of internet
  • Data packets routed through multiple paths

WiFi Technology

  • Uses IEEE 802.11 standards
  • Creates local wireless networks
  • Secured with WPA2/WPA3 encryption
  • Range: 30-100 meters typically

Email System

  • Format: username@domain.com
  • Can attach files and documents
  • Stored on servers until read
  • Spam filters protect from junk mail

Key Features

High Speed: Instant data transmission globally
Connectivity: Links billions of people worldwide
Knowledge Access: Vast educational resources available
Real-time Updates: Live information and news
