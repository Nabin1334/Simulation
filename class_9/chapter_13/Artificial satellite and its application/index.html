🛰 Satellite Applications

🌍 Earth Observation
Satellites like Landsat or Sentinel monitor weather patterns, climate change, deforestation, agriculture, and natural disasters. Used by agencies like NASA, ESA, and NOAA for environmental monitoring.
📡 Telecommunications
Systems like Starlink or Intelsat provide TV broadcasting, broadband internet, phone connections, and global networks. Crucial for remote areas, maritime, and aviation communications.
🧭 Navigation (GPS)
Constellations like GPS (USA), GLONASS (Russia), Galileo (EU), and BeiDou (China) enable precise positioning for vehicles, smartphones, aircraft, ships, and timing for financial systems.
🔬 Scientific Research
Satellites like Hubble or ISS study cosmic phenomena, Earth's magnetic field, atmospheric composition, space weather, and conduct microgravity experiments in biology and physics.
🛰 Military & Defense
Used for surveillance, reconnaissance, early missile warning, secure communications, and intelligence gathering to support national security operations worldwide.
🌤 Weather Forecasting
GEO satellites like GOES or Meteosat monitor atmospheric conditions, track storms, predict weather patterns, and provide early warnings for hurricanes, floods, and other natural disasters.
