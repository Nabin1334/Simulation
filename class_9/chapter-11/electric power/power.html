Circuit Formulas

Ohm's Law
I = V ÷ R
Current equals Voltage divided by Resistance.
Electric Power
P = V × I
Power = Voltage × Current (Watts).
Power Dissipation
P = I² × R
Heat power in resistor.
Energy Consumed
E = P × t
Energy (Joules) = Power × Time.
