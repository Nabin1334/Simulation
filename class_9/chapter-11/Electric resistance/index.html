🎓 What is Electric Resistance?

Resistance is anything that makes it harder for electricity to flow through a wire. Think of it like this: when water flows through a pipe, a narrow section slows down the water. In the same way, resistance slows down the flow of electricity (current).

The Golden Rule (Ohm's Law):

More Resistance = Less Current
Less Resistance = More Current

The formula is: Current (I) = Voltage (V) ÷ Resistance (R)
