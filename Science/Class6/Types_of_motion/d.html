🚗 LINEAR MOTION - Car on Straight Road

Linear Motion

Linear Motion is motion along a straight line with constant direction.

s = ut + ½at²

📌 Key Features:

  • Straight path (no curvature)
  • Velocity vector constant
  • No centripetal force

Curvilinear Motion

Circular Motion — direction changes continuously → centripetal acceleration.

ac = v² / r    Fc = m·v²/r

🎯 Real World Examples:

  • Car on curved/roundabout
  • Planets orbiting Sun
  • Race track banking
