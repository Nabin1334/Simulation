Linear Motion

Linear motion is when an object moves in a straight line. The path is direct without any curves.

Examples:

  • A car moving straight on a highway
  • A ball falling straight down
  • A person walking in a straight line
  • A train on straight tracks
  • An elevator moving up and down

Curvilinear Motion

Curvilinear motion is when an object moves along a curved path. The direction changes continuously.

Examples:

  • A roller coaster going through loops
  • A planet orbiting the sun
  • A basketball arcing toward the hoop
  • A swing moving back and forth
  • A car turning around a corner

Comparison of Motion Types

Feature Linear Motion Curvilinear Motion
Path Shape Straight line Curved line
Direction Change No change in direction Continuous change in direction
Speed Can be constant or changing Can be constant or changing
