Anomalous Expansion

The Rule Breaker

Almost all liquids contract (shrink) as they get colder. Water does this too, but only until it hits 4°C.

The Anomaly (4°C to 0°C)

Below 4°C, water starts to expand as it cools down to 0°C. This makes colder water (like 1°C or 0°C) lighter (less dense) than 4°C water.

Why does Ice Float?

Because of this expansion, ice (solid water) is about 9% less dense than liquid water. This is why ice floats on top of lakes, insulating the fish below!
