How It Works

  1. Enter two decimal numbers (up to 3 decimal places)
  2. Click "Add Decimals" to see the visualization
  3. Each number is represented using Base 10 blocks
  4. Blocks are combined and regrouped when needed
  5. The final result is shown after regrouping
  6. Use "Zoom In" and "Zoom Out" to adjust the visualization size
