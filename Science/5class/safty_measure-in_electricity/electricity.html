Electrical Safety Guide

Stay Safe Around Electricity!

Electricity is very important in our daily life, but if not used carefully, it can cause life-threatening accidents. Let's learn how to stay safe around electricity!

Keep Away from Sockets

Never insert fingers or objects into electrical outlets. They are not toys!

⚡
Status: Safe

Cover Bare Wires

Always cover bare wires with electrical tape. Exposed wires can cause shocks.

⚡
Status: Protected

Wear Protective Gear

Always wear rubber gloves when doing electrical work. Rubber doesn't conduct electricity.

⚡
Status: Protected

Emergency Procedure

In case of a power cut, lightning, or fire, the main switch should be turned off immediately!

If something electrical breaks, always call an adult or an electrician to fix it.

Do's ✅

  • Ask an adult for help with electrical devices
  • Keep electrical devices away from water
  • Tell an adult if you see damaged wires
  • Unplug devices by holding the plug, not the cord
  • Call an electrician if something breaks
  • Wear rubber gloves for electrical work
  • Cover bare wires with electrical tape

Don'ts ❌

  • Don't insert fingers into plugs or sockets
  • Don't use electrical devices with wet hands
  • Don't touch broken electrical equipment
  • Don't overload sockets with too many plugs
  • Don't fly kites near power lines
  • Don't climb trees near power lines
  • Don't use damaged electrical cords

Remember: Electricity is helpful but can be dangerous. Always be careful and ask an adult for help!
