Understanding Digital Wellbeing

What is Digital Wellbeing?

Digital wellbeing skills are necessary to have awareness of the possible effects of the unnecessary use of digital technology. It helps maintain a balanced relationship with technology and prevents addiction.

Long-term Effects

  • • Chaotic lifestyle
  • • Digital addiction
  • • Reduced productivity
  • • Sleep disturbances

Solutions

  • ✓ Separate screen time
  • ✓ Set time limits for social media
  • ✓ Turn off mobile notifications at work
  • ✓ Use digital wellbeing apps
