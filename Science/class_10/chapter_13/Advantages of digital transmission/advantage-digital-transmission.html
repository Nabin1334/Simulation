📡 Advantages of Digital Transmission

Why Digital is Better
🛡️

Noise Immunity
Ignores static interference

Noise Level:
ANALOG

Noise ruins the signal, causing static and distortion that can't be removed.

DIGITAL

Only reads 0s and 1s. Small noise doesn't change the data at all.

🔄

Regeneration
Perfect copies every time

Original
Copy 1
ANALOG

Each generation gets worse and blurrier. Quality degrades quickly.

DIGITAL

The 100th copy is still perfect. There is zero loss of information.

💾

Storage
Fits more in less space

🎵 Song 1
🎵 Song 2
🎵 Song 3
ANALOG

Bulky and heavy. One song needs one whole cassette tape.

DIGITAL

Thousands of songs fit on a single fingernail-sized SD card.

🔒

Security
Keep messages private

NEPAL
ANALOG

Anyone can "tune in" and listen. Very hard to keep private.

DIGITAL

Can be locked with encryption codes that only you know.

✅

Error Detection
Finds and fixes mistakes

HELP
ANALOG

No way to know if an error occurred during transmission.

DIGITAL

System automatically detects a "flipped bit" and fixes it.
