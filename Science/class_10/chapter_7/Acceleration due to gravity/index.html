Effects of Anomalous Expansion
🧊Ice Floats on Water
Freezing expands water ~9%. Ice density = 0.917 g/cm³, less than liquid water. So ice floats, insulating lakes.
🐟Aquatic Life Survives Winter
Densest water (4°C) sinks to lake bottom. Ice forms on top. Fish survive in warm layer below ice all winter.
⭐Maximum Density at 4°C
Water is densest at exactly 4°C. Both cooling below AND heating above cause expansion — unique to water!
💥Burst Water Pipes in Winter
Freezing water expands with ~25,000 psi force, bursting metal and plastic pipes in cold climates.
🗿Weathering of Rocks
Water in rock cracks freezes, expands, splits the rock. Repeated freeze-thaw cycles shape mountains into soil.
♨️Boiling & Steam Power
At 100°C water becomes steam, expanding ~1700×. Latent heat = 2260 J/g. Powers steam engines & turbines.
