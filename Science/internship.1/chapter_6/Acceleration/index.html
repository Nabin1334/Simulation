Smooth Deceleration

Gradual Stopping

The Stop button sets target speed to 0, but the bus slows down gradually at 2.5 m/s² - just like a real bus!

Stopping time = Speed ÷ 2.5

Example

At 60 km/h (16.7 m/s):
Stop time = 16.7 ÷ 2.5 = 6.7 seconds
Stop distance = 56 meters

Try This

Set speed to 60 km/h, click Stop, and watch the speed decrease smoothly over 6-7 seconds!
