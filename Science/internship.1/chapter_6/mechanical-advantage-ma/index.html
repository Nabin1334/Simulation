What is Mechanical Advantage?

Mechanical Advantage (MA) tells you how many times a lever multiplies your force.

MA = Load ÷ Effort

• MA > 1: Makes work easier (force multiplier)

• MA = 1: No advantage

• MA < 1: Makes work harder (but may increase speed)

Example: If MA = 2, you need half the force!
