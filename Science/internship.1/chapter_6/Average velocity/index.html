Understanding Real-Time Velocity

Real-Time Measurement

The clock counts up as the car moves, showing exactly how much time has passed during the race.

Instant Velocity

Velocity is calculated in real-time using the current distance and elapsed time.

Visual Connection

See the direct relationship: as time increases, the car moves further along the track.

Real-Time Calculation:

At any moment during the race:

Velocity = Current Distance ÷ Current Time
