🌐 How WiFi transfers data

🔹 Radio waves (blue circles) carry information through the air — like invisible messengers.

🔹 Files, photos, or videos are broken into tiny data packets (red-bordered boxes). Each packet flies from your phone to the router, then to the laptop.

🔹 The receiving device sends back a tiny ACK (acknowledgment) packet to say "got it!" — that's how WiFi makes sure nothing gets lost.

  • 📱 Phone → 📶 Router → 💻 Laptop
  • 💻 Laptop → 📶 Router → 📱 Phone
  • ✅ ACK confirms delivery
