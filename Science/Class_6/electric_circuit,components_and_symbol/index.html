Conducting Wires

Provides a path for electrons to flow. Made of conductive materials like copper.

Instructions

  • Click the "Toggle Switch" button or click directly on the switch in the diagram to open/close the circuit.
  • When the circuit is closed, current flows and the bulb lights up.
  • When the circuit is open, no current flows and the bulb remains off.
  • Change the voltage to see how it affects current (Ohm's Law: I = V/R).
  • Hover over components to learn more about their function.
