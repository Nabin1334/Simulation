How Computers Work

Input Devices send information to the computer. Examples: keyboard, mouse, microphone.

Output Devices receive information from the computer. Examples: monitor, printer, speakers.

The System Unit (CPU) processes information from input devices and sends it to output devices.

Mass Storage Devices store information for later use.
