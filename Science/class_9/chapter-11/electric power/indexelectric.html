Circuit Formulas

Ohm's Law
I = V ÷ R
Current (I) equals Voltage (V) divided by Resistance (R). This is the fundamental relationship in electrical circuits.
Electric Power
P = V × I
Power (P) is the rate at which electrical energy is transferred by the circuit, measured in Watts.
Power Dissipation
P = I² × R
Power dissipated as heat in a resistor equals the square of current multiplied by resistance.
Energy Consumed
E = P × t
Energy (E) consumed equals Power (P) multiplied by Time (t). Energy is measured in Joules.

How to Use This Simulator

  • Adjust Voltage using the slider or preset buttons to change the battery voltage (1.5V to 24V)
  • Adjust Resistance using the slider or preset buttons to change the resistor value (1Ω to 30Ω)
  • Click ON/OFF Button to turn the circuit on or off
  • When ON, observe electrons flowing and the bulb lighting up
  • Observe how current changes when you modify voltage or resistance
  • Watch the power value - higher power means the bulb glows brighter
  • See energy accumulate over time when circuit is ON
  • Real-life analogy: Voltage is like water pressure, current is like water flow, and resistance is like pipe thickness
