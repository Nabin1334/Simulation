Real Factors Affecting Resistance
Length of Conductor
1.0 m
Short Long
Physics Relationship: R ∝ L (Direct Proportionality)
Longer wires provide more obstacles for electrons to collide with atomic structures, increasing resistance proportionally. Double the length = Double the resistance.
Cross-sectional Area
0.785 mm²
Thin Thick
Physics Relationship: R ∝ 1/A (Inverse Proportionality)
Thicker wires provide more pathways (cross-sectional area) for electrons to flow through, reducing resistance. Double the area = Half the resistance.

Temperature Formula: Rₜ = R₂₀[1 + 0.0039×(T-20)]
Higher temperature increases atomic vibrations, creating more obstacles for electrons. For copper, resistance increases by 0.39% per °C above 20°C.
