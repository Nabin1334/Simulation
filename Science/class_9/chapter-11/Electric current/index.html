Instructions

  • Click directly on the switch in the circuit diagram to open/close it.
  • Click on the bulb to change its brightness level (resistance changes).
  • When circuit closed, current flows and bulb lights up.
  • Open circuit: no current, bulb off.
  • Change voltage to see Ohm's Law in action (I = V/R).
