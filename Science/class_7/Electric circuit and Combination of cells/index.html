Electric Circuit Experiment

Effect of Number of Cells on Bulb Brightness - Series Connection

Materials Required

🔌 Conducting Wire
🔋 Few Dry Cells
💡 Bulb
⚡ Switch

Procedure

  1. Connect bulbs in the circuits of one cell, two cells and three cells as shown in the circuits below.
  2. Click the Toggle Switch button to open/close each circuit.
  3. Observe the brightness of the bulb in each circuit configuration.
  4. Notice how adding more cells in series increases the bulb brightness.
