How to Use This App:

1. Type your message in the input box and click "Send" or press Enter

2. Watch the 3D characters react when messages are sent and received

3. The characters will bounce when new messages arrive

4. Use "Switch User" to change who you're chatting as

5. Have fun communicating with your friends!
