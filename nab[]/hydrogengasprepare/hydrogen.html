Laboratory Preparation of Hydrogen Gas

Interactive Simulation of the Reaction Between Zinc and Dilute Acid

About the Experiment

This simulation demonstrates the laboratory preparation of hydrogen gas by reacting granulated zinc with dilute hydrochloric acid. The chemical reaction is:

Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂↑

Apparatus Components

Woulfe's Bottle
Thistle Funnel
Granulated Zinc
Dilute Acid
Delivery Tube
Water Trough
Gas Jar
Beehive Shelf

How It Works

When dilute hydrochloric acid is added to granulated zinc in the Woulfe's bottle, hydrogen gas is produced. The gas travels through the delivery tube and is collected in the gas jar by downward displacement of water.

Click "Start Reaction" to begin the simulation and observe the production of hydrogen gas bubbles.
